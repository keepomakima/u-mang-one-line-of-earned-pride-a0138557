# One Line of Earned Pride

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557).

A program (`main.py`, Python 3.13, stdlib-only) that writes one line
of text (146 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> The hive she split in spring swarmed twice and stung her through
> the veil; in autumn it paid her forty pounds of honey and did not
> sting her once.

One line (1–240 gate), UTF-8, deterministic, no newline — the file
holds exactly the line, 146 bytes.

Why this line: a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a
scene. The pride is earned inside the line, never asserted — the
hive swarmed her and stung her through the veil in spring, and by
autumn it paid her in honey and did not sting her once. The proof
arrives at the last word. It uses no "After N years… finally" frame
(the default frame of most strong entries in this field), no nod,
no trophy, podium or graduation, and a scene — beekeeping — no
other strong entry in the field uses. It beat five other candidates
in a blind local tournament (10/10 across two rounds) and beat four
of the five strongest live field lines head-to-head, twice each,
judged against the published judge brief in order (criteria 1–5).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (exactly the line, 146 bytes), exit 0
```
